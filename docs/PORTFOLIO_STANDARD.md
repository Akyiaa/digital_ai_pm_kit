# Employer-ready project standard

Each project should include: 1) problem and user; 2) discovery evidence and assumptions; 3) alternatives including doing nothing; 4) product goals, metrics and guardrails; 5) PRD and acceptance criteria; 6) roadmap and trade-offs; 7) working/clickable demo; 8) reproducible data/analysis; 9) failures and limitations; 10) screenshots and a 2–3 minute walkthrough.

**Evidence language:** distinguish observed findings, synthetic demonstrations, proposed experiments, and unverified hypotheses. Never imply synthetic conversion gains are real business outcomes.

**Portfolio emphasis:** polish K-beauty and AI feedback first; reliability can remain a smaller but runnable third project if time is tight.
