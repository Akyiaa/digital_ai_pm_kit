# Pre-publication safety checklist

- [ ] All code, prompts, datasets, diagrams and screenshots were independently produced or explicitly licensed for publication.
- [ ] No Amex/customer/cardmember data, incident details, logs, internal architecture, tickets, code, prompts, model outputs, pricing, performance findings, or credentials.
- [ ] No personal or third-party confidential information; interviews used informed consent and anonymised notes.
- [ ] Work-device, work-account and employer-tool outputs are **not transferred** unless policy and authorisation explicitly permit publication.
- [ ] Synthetic data and hypothetical business assumptions are labelled.
- [ ] Third-party images/assets and AI-generated content comply with licence/terms.
- [ ] `git status` and diff checked; no secrets, `.env`, private CVs or application tracker.

Safest approach: recreate all public artifacts independently on a personal device using synthetic/public data. Even anonymised work findings can remain confidential or employer-owned.
